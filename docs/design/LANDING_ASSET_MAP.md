## Interactive experience

The chamber artwork sits beneath live signing traces. The ledger uses a separate etched substrate in its header. Both are original ImageGen renders delivered as WebP. The diagrams use SVG paths that stay legible when images are unavailable. The original static design is preserved at commit 2570d72. The /overview route offers a text-led fallback with the bilingual wallet capture and the same Pudge links.

The spending example assumes an approved recipient and a separate eligible fee input. It demonstrates the 61 CKB payment and per-payment cap, shared and personal allowances, revocation, and the 73 CKB protected reserve. Recovery figures are based on the historical November 15 closeout and show returned capacity before fees.
