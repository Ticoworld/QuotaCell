## Capacity budget and boundary

For one example with a new standard-lock Q code cell, one active policy, one protected fund large enough for two minimum 61 CKB recipient outputs while retaining a 73 CKB fund, one owner fee Cell and two delegate fee Cells, the minimum locked/output capacity is: Q code 23,661 + policy 600 + fund 195 + owner fee Cell 61 + delegate fee Cells 122 = **24,639 CKB**, plus deployment, funding/distribution, lifecycle and late-deposit fees, plus any desired spending above the two minimum outputs and a fee reserve. The recorded isolated lifecycle used 2,000 shannons per transaction (eight total 0.00016 CKB); that is not a current Pudge fee quote and does not include deployment or distribution. Reusing the verified live A/F cells avoids their 1,277 + 27,421 CKB standard-lock code-cell capacities; licensing remains a separate requirement. Late funding needs at least 73 CKB and can reuse recovered owner funds. Closure returns 466 of the 600 policy CKB and retains 134. The full actual required input and fees depend on live cells, transaction sizes and current fee rate; no funding sufficiency is claimed.

No Pudge contract deployment or lifecycle transaction was attempted. Pudge execution requires, at minimum, complete K/Q dependency verification, public-chain discovery and submission/confirmation behavior, disposable testnet capacity and explicit real wallet approvals. Uncertain payments must not be automatically retried with new inputs or signatures.
